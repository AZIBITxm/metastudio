About Us

Zanders Atelier is a team of professionals with more than ten years of experience in interior design and furniture making, delivering exceptional solutions across Poland and Western Europe. Our long-standing expertise allows us to offer the highest quality of service and turn our clients’ dreams of unique, functional spaces into reality.

We specialise in built-in furniture for homes, restaurants and public buildings, offering a complete service, from concept to completion.

"Furniture is not just an object, it's part of your life."

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that furniture is not merely an object but an essential part of your life. That is why every design is created around your needs, comfort and style. We combine modern technology with elegance and functionality to deliver solutions that fully meet your expectations.

We approach every project individually, with full commitment and passion. We offer a free online consultation, during which you can see a visualisation of your dream furniture and explore your room in 3D.

Thanks to modern furniture-making technology and attention to every detail, we deliver products of the highest quality that delight our clients with innovative solutions.

Furniture Boards

Versatile composite materials from leading European manufacturers such as Pfleiderer, Swiss Krono, Kronospan and Egger. We offer laminated boards with high resistance to scratches, moisture and heat, as well as high-density HDF boards with a smooth surface ideal for further finishing. Every board guarantees dimensional stability and long-lasting functionality.

Furniture Fronts

The finishing elements of cabinets and furniture that define their appearance and functionality. We offer a full range of solutions: acrylic fronts with exceptional resistance to mechanical damage, lacquered fronts with fully customisable colours, veneered fronts combining the beauty of natural wood with stability, solid wood fronts, glass fronts with aluminium frames, curved fronts with rounded edges and milled fronts with precise patterns. Every front is tailored to the specific requirements of each room.

Worktops

Functional work surfaces that are also a key decorative element giving character to the entire interior. We offer laminate worktops with high-pressure HPL laminate imitating the texture of wood, stone or concrete, exceptionally hard sintered quartz made of 90-95% natural quartzite, artistic epoxy resin worktops with unlimited customisation options, solid wood worktops creating a warm atmosphere, luxurious granite and marble worktops and innovative Solid Surface worktops allowing seamless joints. Every material guarantees durability, easy maintenance and an outstanding visual effect.

Hinges

Hinges decide how cabinet doors open: closing softly, opening wide in a corner, without blocking inner drawers, completely invisibly, or with the front sliding away into the side of the cabinet.

Handle-free opening

Handle-free fronts open with a push, with a light touch assisted by a small motor, or through a finger recess in an aluminium profile. The furniture looks calmer and is easier to keep clean.

Drawers

Drawer systems differ in side thickness and shape, height and finish. Each can have soft-close or push-to-open (see “Drawers — options”).

Drawers — options

Extras that suit any drawer system: opening method, soft-close, heights and interior organisation.

Lift systems for wall cabinets

Lift systems raise the fronts of wall cabinets upwards instead of swinging them sideways. The type of movement is chosen to match the front size and cabinet height.

Pull-outs, larders and baskets

Pull-out baskets and shelves use every centimetre of the cabinet — from narrow spice pull-outs to tall larders and waste sorting under the sink.

Corner solutions

The corner is the hardest place in a kitchen. These fittings bring the contents out of the depth of the cabinet to you.

Plinths

The space behind the plinth under base cabinets usually goes to waste — it can hold a flat drawer or a pull-out step.

Pull-down shelves, lifts and tables

Mechanisms that bring the contents of a cabinet to a comfortable height or pull an extra worktop out of it.

Sockets and charging

Power where you need it — without sockets on show or cables on the worktop.

LED lighting

Light inside furniture: above the worktop, on shelves, in display cabinets and inside drawers and wardrobes.

Wardrobe

Fittings and accessories that organise a wardrobe or walk-in closet: rails, pull-out hangers, drawer inserts, shoe racks, laundry baskets, mirrors, display fronts and lighting.

Sliding, folding and pocket doors

Door systems for wardrobes and built-in alcoves that need no space for a swinging door.

Smart solutions

A selection of interesting and new solutions — including unbranded products from China. We show them as inspiration; before installation we check availability and compliance with standards (CE).
