About Us

Zanders Atelier is a team of professionals with more than ten years of experience in interior design and furniture making, delivering exceptional solutions across Poland and Western Europe. Our long-standing expertise allows us to offer the highest quality of service and turn our clients’ dreams of unique, functional spaces into reality.

Furniture Boards

Versatile composite materials from leading European manufacturers such as Pfleiderer, Swiss Krono, Kronospan and Egger. We offer laminated boards with high resistance to scratches, moisture and heat, as well as high-density HDF boards with a smooth surface ideal for further finishing. Every board guarantees dimensional stability and long-lasting functionality.

Laminated Boards

Laminated boards are versatile composite materials consisting of a core board (chipboard or MDF) covered on both sides with a decorative melamine laminate. They are highly resistant to scratches, moisture and high temperatures. Thanks to the latest production technology they are more durable than lacquered boards and are ideal for kitchen and bathroom furniture.

Properties:

  • Standard thickness: 18mm (also available: 10mm, 12mm, 16mm, 22mm, 25mm, 28mm)
  • Standard format: 2800 x 2070mm or 2750 x 2100mm
  • Resistant to scratches and chipping
  • Easy to keep clean
  • Wide range of decors and textures

Applications:

  • ✅ Kitchen furniture carcasses and fronts
  • ✅ Bathroom furniture (using boards with increased moisture resistance)
  • ✅ Wardrobes, walk-in closets and bedroom furniture
  • ✅ Office furniture (desks, pedestals, shelving)
  • ✅ Children's and youth furniture
  • ✅ Built-in alcoves and wall panels

HDF Boards (High-Density Fibreboard)

A high-density fibreboard made from wood fibres pressed under very high pressure and temperature. Thanks to its compact structure it is considerably harder and more resistant to bending than standard MDF.

Smooth surface ideal for lacquering and veneering

📐 Technical Specification

Standard thickness: 3mm, 4mm, 5mm
Density: Over 800 kg/m³
Surface: Smooth and uniform
Features: High hardness, dimensional stability, good acoustic insulation

🎯 Practical Applications

Structural Elements
  • ✅ Furniture backs (wardrobe and shelving back panels)
  • ✅ Drawer bottoms
  • ✅ Upholstered furniture components
Finishing & Decoration
  • ✅ Interior door panels
  • ✅ Underlay for floor panels
  • ✅ Decorative elements
Special applications: Small wooden goods • Precision components • Structural underlays

Laminate Worktops

Made of furniture board (e.g. MDF, chipboard) covered with high-pressure laminate (HPL). Modern laminates imitate the texture and pattern of wood, stone or concrete almost perfectly, while offering excellent resistance to abrasion, stains and high temperatures.

Technical Specifications

  • Standard thicknesses: 28mm and 38mm (other variants to order)
  • Base material: MDF board or chipboard
  • Coating: High-pressure laminate (HPL)
  • Properties: Resistant to abrasion, stains and high temperatures
Edge types:
  • Straight: Standard, economical solution
  • Profiled: With a decorative rounded edge (e.g. "C" or "S" shaped)
  • Made to measure: Any shape can be milled, including curves

Applications

  • ✅ Kitchens (excellent stain resistance)
  • ✅ Offices and public buildings
  • ✅ Bathrooms (using moisture-resistant board)
  • ✅ Economical and fast projects
  • ⚠️ Avoid cutting directly on the surface and contact with fire

Sintered Quartz Worktops

Modern sintered quartz is made of approx. 90-95% natural ground quartzite and quartz bound with resins. It is extremely hard and resistant to scratches, stains, acids and high temperatures. It requires no maintenance.

Technical Specifications

  • Composition: 90-95% natural quartzite and quartz + resins
  • Standard thicknesses: 12mm and 20mm
  • Edge finishing: Can be thickened, as with stone
Surface types:
  • Smooth gloss: Striking, easy to clean, enhances the depth of colour
  • Matt (soft, satin, velvet): Velvety to the touch, hides minor scratches
  • Textured/porous ("Rock", "Terra"): Textures imitating raw stone or concrete while remaining highly stain-resistant
Advantages of sintered quartz:
  • • Non-porous – excellent hygiene, stain resistance
  • • Exceptional durability and hardness – resistant to scratches and impacts
  • • A wealth of patterns and colours – from plain to marble and concrete effects
  • • Maintenance-free – no sealing required

Applications

  • ✅ Kitchens (ideal around the sink and hob)
  • ✅ Bathrooms (vanity tops, window sills, shower enclosures)
  • ✅ Areas requiring the highest hygiene
  • ✅ Commercial interiors (reception desks, bars, tables)
  • ✅ Projects demanding exceptional design

Solid Surface Worktops (e.g. Corian, Hi-Macs, Staron)

An innovative composite material made of approx. 2/3 natural minerals and 1/3 acrylic resin. Its greatest advantage is that it is seamless – joints are invisible, creating a uniform, monolithic surface.

Technical Specifications

  • Composition: 2/3 natural minerals + 1/3 acrylic resin
  • Sheet thickness: 12mm (bonded to an MDF base)
  • Final worktop thickness: From 30mm to 50mm or more
  • Thermoforming: Can be formed into any shape
Key features and advantages:
  • Seamless and monolithic: A perfectly smooth surface with an integrated sink is possible
  • 3D forming: Rounded fronts, gentle slopes, non-standard shapes
  • Non-porous: The surface is hygienic and resistant to stains and bacterial growth
  • Damage resistance: Minor scratches can be removed by sanding
  • Certificates: Often certified for food contact

Applications

  • ✅ Hospitality (restaurants, bars, hotel kitchens)
  • ✅ Hospitals and medical facilities (for hygiene reasons)
  • ✅ Luxury kitchens and bathrooms (projects requiring integrated sinks)
  • ✅ Surfaces requiring seamless solutions
